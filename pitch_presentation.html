Methodology
04

The 3-Layer Score Engine

We do not rely on a single metric; instead, we analyze structural relationships.

01

Market Dynamics (Volume vs. FDV)

We cross-reference 24h trading volume against Fully Diluted Valuation. A chronically low ratio indicates heavy supply overhang and 'ghost chain' properties.

02

Liquidity Exhaustion

If less than 1% of the asset's value is backed by actual DEX/CEX liquidity, a death spiral is imminent upon any sell pressure.

03

Structural Decay (Drawdown Matrix)

We evaluate continuous drawdown over 7d, 30d, 60d, and 90d periods. Persistent decay without mean reversion confirms terminal holder capitulation.
